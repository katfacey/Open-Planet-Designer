# AI disclosure

Open Planet Designer was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex. AI assistance contributed to code, Blender node construction, asset-processing scripts, research, documentation and debugging.

The beta demo consists of actual Blender renders. It is not an AI-generated depiction of an unimplemented product. The supplied vegetation and scanned surfaces come from the credited CC0 sources; regional elevation comes from public-domain USGS data. The disclaimer does not change those assets' licenses or imply that their creators endorse this project.

The Godot migration code, exporter and documentation also use substantial AI assistance. Its documented captures are actual Godot renders of imported Blender geometry. The initial import has frozen coverage; procedural world streaming, planetary atmospheric entry and photographic calibration remain development goals.

AI-assisted development can introduce mistakes. This is experimental beta software: tests cover specific cases, not every scene, device or Blender version. Save a copy of your scene before using it, and report reproducible problems with your Blender version and hardware. Photorealism, continuous close-detail scalability and near-matching Eevee/Cycles images are project goals, not universal guarantees.
