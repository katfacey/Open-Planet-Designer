# AI disclosure

Open Planet Designer was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI Codex. AI assistance contributed to code, Blender node construction, asset-processing scripts, research, documentation and debugging.

The beta demo consists of actual Blender renders. It is not an AI-generated depiction of an unimplemented product. The supplied vegetation and scanned surfaces come from the credited CC0 sources; regional elevation comes from public-domain USGS data. The disclaimer does not change those assets' licenses or imply that their creators endorse this project.

The Godot code, exporters and documentation also use substantial AI assistance. Its documented captures are actual Godot renders. The initial import retains frozen Blender coverage; the native 0.2 alpha adds procedural terrain/ecosystem streaming using the retained source assets and regional data. Planetary atmospheric entry, photographic calibration, complete temporal LOD and qualified hardware presets remain development goals.

The project remains experimental: documented checks cover specific configurations. Bug reports should include the Godot or Blender version, hardware, settings and reproduction steps. Photorealism, seamless close-detail scalability and consumer hardware targets remain unqualified. Archived Eevee/Cycles comparisons are historical research.
